    How to read this

    Fit is the collector's own confidence that an advert wants the engagement we sell (contract, fractional, part-time) for engineering work we do. Strong means both are stated plainly; thin means the evidence is partial or ambiguous. It measures the rules, not the company. Only replies will tell us whether the rules pick the right ones.

    No emails or names are collected into this page on purpose. The collector never builds a contact list; the advert link is the route, and who gets contacted is your decision.

    Agency marks a company hiring contractors for its own client projects ("for our clients"). It may buy white-label engineering, but it is not the company with the problem. Recruiters placing someone with an unnamed client are already left out.

    Older adverts: more buyer adverts are older than days. Most of those roles are likely filled, so they are left out here; they stay in the archive.
